# Bilaro

Goal: Swiss SMEs move from bexio to Bilaro and pay for it

Stations (in this order):
1. Core bookkeeping (journal, receipts, bank, VAT, statements, invoices, dunning)
2. bexio takeover (import from bexio export files, balances by cut-off date)
3. Year-end & VAT (closing assistant, VAT reconciliation and XML, fixed assets)
4. AI connector (Claude connector with read tools and confirmed write tools)
5. Tester-ready (feedback rounds fixed, design on the level of the best tools)
6. Paying customers (own company's books in Bilaro, first SMEs fully off bexio, public pricing and sign-up)

Notes:
- Billing, roles and two-factor sign-in belong to Core bookkeeping.
- No longer focused on garages; Swiss SMEs in general.
