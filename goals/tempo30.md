# Tempo 30

Goal: Tempo30 is on Steam: a funny multiplayer arcade racer in Zurich that players buy

Stations (in this order):
1. Core driving (vehicles, handling, races, AI drivers)
2. Zurich city (drivable Zurich, landmarks, street dressing; final map about 15 to 20 km)
3. Traffic & police (traffic, trams, buses, pedestrians, chases, fines)
4. Multiplayer (rooms, shared traffic, PC and Mac together)
5. Public reveal (Steam page, first Reddit post, demo build)
6. Early Access (paid release on Steam with many districts, English and German)
