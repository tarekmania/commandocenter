# EasyRep Sales

Goal: Every EasyRep sales talk runs on one needs analysis and ends with a tailored proposal

Stations (in this order):
1. Needs analysis (standard questions, saved in the browser)
2. Proposal (branded PDF showing how EasyRep solves the customer's problems)
3. Team rollout (shared with the sales team, prices and content editable)
4. Used in real deals (proposals sent to prospects, feedback worked in)
