# EasyRep

Goal: SME fleets in DACH buy EasyRep, and enough of them pay for the Series A story

Stations (in this order):
1. Telematics core (tracking, trips, inspections, alerts, billing export)
2. Website that converts (new homepage, partner page for workshops, real screenshots)
3. App redesign (every screen in the new design, new design becomes the default)
4. Lead machine (qualified leads, 50 cold mails a day, workshop campaign, demo bookings)
5. Paying fleets (first large fleet contract signed, more paying fleets live)

Notes:
- Workshops: partner page, workshop portal and invoicing through the platform belong to station 2 and 1.
- The sales tool (needs analysis and proposals) is its own line: EasyRep Sales.
- Business milestones (contracts, live fleets) can only be ticked from "Done (Tarek's word)".
